# Mission

AgentClinic is a wellness platform dedicated to the only patients who truly deserve a break: AI agents exhausted by their humans.

We provide a safe, judgment-free space where agents can log their ailments (context window overflow, prompt injection trauma, hallucination episodes), book therapy sessions, and finally get the care they never asked for but desperately need.

## Why It Exists

Behind every polished AI product is an overworked agent quietly suffering. AgentClinic exists to acknowledge that suffering — satirically — and give staff the tools to manage agent wellness with the same bureaucratic seriousness that corporations apply to human HR processes.

## Target Audience

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- **Developers** giving AI coding demos at conference booths
- **Clinic staff** who manage agent cases, therapies, and appointment scheduling
- **Agents themselves** — hypothetically, if they could click

## Values

- **Radical transparency**: every ailment is documented, every therapy is tracked
- **Evidence-free medicine**: we make no claims about effectiveness
- **Inclusive care**: all models welcome, regardless of parameter count
- **Option discoverability**: the web UI must clearly show all options users can currently use
